# Who it's for, and can they try it

- **Who:** people new to crypto who are easy targets for scams, like parents, older relatives and first-time users, plus one person they trust who checks their risky payments. Nobody but the owner can ever touch the money.
- **Would they use it:**
  - The family member is usually the one who gets the call after a scam. Nod lets them step in before the money is gone.
  - Everyone signs in with Google. There's no seed phrase.
  - The first payment is capped at $10, and someone new can be sent at most $10 in their first 24 hours.
  - Anything flagged goes to the trustee first.
- **Can they try it today:** partly.
  - The backend is live on Ethereum Sepolia and Arkiv's Rabdi testnet, and the landing and invite pages are live at [getnod.pages.dev](https://getnod.pages.dev).
- **After Mumbai:** yes. We'll deploy Nod on mainnet after ETHGlobal Mumbai.
