# Who Nod is for

## Who it's for

People who are new to crypto and easy targets for scams, like parents, older relatives and first-time users, plus one person they trust who checks their risky payments. That trusted person can never touch the money. Only its owner can.

## Would they use it

- The family member is usually the one who gets the phone call after a scam. Nod lets them step in before the money is gone.
- Everyone signs in with Google. There's no seed phrase to write down or lose.
- The first payment is capped at $10, and someone new can be sent at most $10 in their first 24 hours.
- Anything that looks risky goes to the trustee before it can be sent.

## Can they try it today

Partly. The backend is live on Ethereum Sepolia and Arkiv's Rabdi testnet, and the landing and invite pages are live at [getnod.pages.dev](https://getnod.pages.dev).

## Will we keep building it

Yes. After ETHGlobal Mumbai we're taking Nod to mainnet.
